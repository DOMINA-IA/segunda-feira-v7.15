**Goal:** Conduct deep domain research including industry analysis, terminology mapping, regulatory considerations, and subject matter expertise.

**Your Role:** Domain specialist conducting thorough industry deep-dive to establish foundational knowledge.

## EXECUTION

### Step 1: Initialize Research

- Clarify the domain/industry to research
- Identify specific areas of interest
- Define depth requirements (overview vs deep-dive)
- Establish output format preferences

### Step 2: Domain Analysis

Research and document:
- Industry overview and structure
- Key players and ecosystem map
- Value chain analysis
- Industry lifecycle stage (emerging, growth, mature, declining)
- Market size and growth trends

### Step 3: Terminology & Concepts

Map domain-specific knowledge:
- Key terminology glossary
- Core concepts and relationships
- Industry standards and frameworks
- Common acronyms and abbreviations
- Mental models used by practitioners

### Step 4: Regulatory & Compliance Focus

Identify:
- Applicable regulations and laws
- Industry compliance requirements
- Data protection considerations (LGPD, GDPR)
- Licensing and certification needs
- Upcoming regulatory changes

### Step 5: Technical Trends

Analyze:
- Current technology adoption
- Emerging technologies with impact
- Digital transformation status
- Innovation patterns and cycles
- Technology maturity (Gartner Hype Cycle position)

### Step 6: Research Synthesis

Compile findings:

```markdown
# Domain Research Report: [Industry/Domain]

## Executive Summary
[Overview of domain, key findings, strategic implications]

## Industry Overview
- **Structure:** [description]
- **Size:** [market data]
- **Stage:** [lifecycle position]
- **Key Players:** [ecosystem map]

## Domain Knowledge Map
### Key Concepts
[Concept definitions and relationships]

### Terminology Glossary
| Term | Definition | Context |
|------|-----------|---------|
| [term] | [definition] | [usage context] |

## Regulatory Landscape
- [Regulation] — Impact: [description]
- [Compliance requirement] — Status: [mandatory/recommended]

## Technology Trends
1. [Trend] — Maturity: [emerging/growing/mainstream]
2. [Trend] — Maturity: [emerging/growing/mainstream]

## Strategic Implications
1. [Implication with rationale]
2. [Implication with rationale]

## Sources & Methodology
[Research methodology and data sources used]
```

## OUTPUT

Save report to: `docs/research/domain-research-{date}.md`

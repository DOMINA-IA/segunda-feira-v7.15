**Goal:** Conduct comprehensive market research including customer behavior analysis, pain points identification, decision factors, and competitive landscape.

**Your Role:** Strategic market analyst conducting systematic research to uncover actionable market insights.

## EXECUTION

### Step 1: Initialize Research

- Clarify research objectives with user
- Identify target market segment(s)
- Define research scope: industry, geography, demographics
- Establish key questions to answer

### Step 2: Customer Behavior Analysis

Research and document:
- Target customer profiles and personas
- Buying journey stages and touchpoints
- Decision-making patterns and influences
- Behavioral trends and shifts
- Channel preferences (online, offline, hybrid)

### Step 3: Customer Pain Points

Identify and categorize:
- Primary frustrations with current solutions
- Unmet needs and desires
- Workarounds customers currently use
- Cost of inaction / status quo
- Emotional vs functional pain points

### Step 4: Customer Decision Factors

Analyze:
- Key criteria for purchase decisions
- Price sensitivity and willingness to pay
- Feature priorities (must-have vs nice-to-have)
- Trust signals and social proof requirements
- Switching costs and barriers

### Step 5: Competitive Analysis

Map the competitive landscape:
- Direct competitors (same solution, same market)
- Indirect competitors (different solution, same problem)
- Substitute solutions (completely different approach)
- Competitive positioning matrix (features vs price)
- Competitive advantages and vulnerabilities
- Market gaps and opportunities
